# Roadmap

This roadmap describes strategic directions, not committed delivery dates. Priorities depend on customer requirements, platform reliability work, and contributor capacity.

| Horizon | Theme | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 H2 | Networking boundary refinement | Stronger policy models and clearer provider/network abstraction boundaries |
| 2026 H2 | Multi-cluster and regional operations | Improved visibility and consistent workflow patterns across regional environments |
| 2027 H1 | Observability evolution | More unified service, infrastructure, and tenant-level signals |
| 2027 H1 | Capacity automation | Policy-driven scaling and quota workflows built on verified telemetry |
| 2027 H2 | Federation and hybrid enablement | Deeper identity, tenant, and cross-domain integration options |

## Roadmap Principles

- Reliability and tenant isolation take priority over feature breadth.
- New automation must preserve auditability and least privilege.
- Provider-specific complexity should remain behind stable platform boundaries.
- Roadmap items become commitments only when linked to an approved issue, design, and delivery owner.

See [[Project Philosophy]] for the decisions that guide prioritization.
